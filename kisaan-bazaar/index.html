Three steps, no detours

Every order follows the same short path from field to doorstep.

1

The farmer lists the harvest

Once a crop is picked, the farmer posts it with quantity, price and the village it came from — updated as often as the harvest changes.

2

You browse and order

Filter by category or search by name, check who grew it, set a quantity and add it to your cart in a couple of taps.
